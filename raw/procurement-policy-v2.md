PROCUREMENT POLICY (v2 - source document)
Purchase Approval Thresholds:
- Under $1,000: requesting manager
- $1,000 - $9,999: department head
- $10,000 - $99,999: finance controller AND department head
- $100,000 and above: CFO
The three-way match (invoice vs PO vs goods receipt) is mandatory for all 
payments regardless of amount.
Verbal pre-approval is never a reason to skip the match.
