The procurement policy sets who must approve a purchase, based on its value, and the controls that must be satisfied before a purchase order is issued. The latest issued text is [Procurement Policy v2](procurement-policy-v2.md); this page collects the operational rules that accompany the thresholds.

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

| Amount (USD) | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Requesting manager |
| $1,000 – $9,999 | Department head |
| $10,000 – $99,999 | Finance controller AND department head |
| $100,000 and above | CFO |

## Key Rules

1. Purchases above $10,000 require **dual approval**: the finance controller and the department head must both sign off before a purchase order is issued.
2. No purchase order may be issued without a valid cost center and a budget check.
3. Emergency purchases above threshold require retroactive approval within 48 hours.
4. The [three-way match](three-way-match.md) (invoice vs PO vs goods receipt) is mandatory for all payments regardless of amount.
