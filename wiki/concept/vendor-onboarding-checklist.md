This checklist is the operational form of the onboarding requirements in [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md). No purchase order may be issued to a vendor until every step is complete.

## Checklist

1. **Legal entity verification and tax ID validation** — confirm the registered entity and its tax identifier.
2. **Bank account verification (anti-fraud check)** — validate bank details independently of the vendor's own submission.
3. **Sanctions and compliance screening** — clear the vendor against sanctions and compliance requirements.
4. **Master data entry with vendor category and payment terms** — record the vendor category and assign payment terms from [Payment Terms](../reference/payment-terms.md).

## Exception Rule

A **bank-detail mismatch requires finance controller review before payment** — the same control applies at onboarding and at every subsequent payment run.
