Version 2 of the procurement policy is the current source document. It restates the value-based approval thresholds and tightens the language around the three-way match: no verbal pre-approval, from any approver, justifies skipping it.

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

- Under $1,000 — requesting manager
- $1,000 – $9,999 — department head
- $10,000 – $99,999 — finance controller **and** department head
- $100,000 and above — CFO

## Mandatory Match Rule

The three-way match (invoice vs PO vs goods receipt) is mandatory for all payments regardless of amount. **Verbal pre-approval is never a reason to skip the match.** See [Three-Way Match](three-way-match.md) and the red-flag guidance in [Payables Fraud Controls](payables-fraud-controls.md).

## Relationship to the Earlier Policy Page

The v2 text covers thresholds and the match rule only. The cost center / budget check requirement and the 48-hour retroactive approval rule for emergency purchases are documented on [Procurement Policy](procurement-policy.md) and are not restated in the v2 source.
